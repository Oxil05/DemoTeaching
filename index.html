LESSON PROPER • PART 3

Universal Logic Gates & Expression Synthesis

NAND and NOR gates are universal building blocks capable of synthesizing any digital circuit.

⚡

Universal Functions

Both NAND and NOR can generate basic AND, OR, and NOT functions.

Sum of Products (SOP)

Implemented using NAND gates only by replacing all AND and OR gates with NAND gates.

Product of Sums (POS)

Implemented using NOR gates only by replacing all AND and OR gates with NOR gates.

🛠️

NAND & NOR Synthesis

NOT A ➔ A NAND A or (A • A)'
A AND B ➔ ((A • B)')'
A OR B ➔ (A' • B')'
